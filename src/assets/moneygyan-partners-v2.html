Questions

Before you apply.

Do I need a finance background?+

No. Many of our strongest partners started outside finance — insurance agents, chartered accountants, retired bank managers, and independent professionals have all built distribution businesses through MoneyGYAN. What matters more is trust with the people around you and the discipline to clear the NISM syllabus.

Is NISM-V-A certification and an ARN mandatory?+

Yes. As per SEBI and AMFI regulations, anyone engaged in selling or marketing mutual fund schemes must pass the NISM-Series-V-A exam and hold a valid AMFI Registration Number (ARN). We guide you through this process; if you already hold a valid ARN from elsewhere, you can move straight to empanelment.

Can I do this part-time?+

Most partners start part-time alongside a job, business, or existing practice like insurance or accounting. It's a relationship-driven business, so consistency matters more than hours.

How and when am I paid?+

Through trail commissions paid by AMCs on assets under management, disbursed as per the standard AMFI-governed commission cycle, directly to your ARN. There are no client fees and no upfront charges to your clients. Commission structures are governed by SEBI/AMFI rules and may change from time to time.

Is there any cost to join MoneyGYAN?+

Joining MoneyGYAN as a partner is free. Standard third-party costs — the NISM exam fee and ARN registration fee set by AMFI — apply as per regulatory norms and are payable to NISM/AMFI directly, not to MoneyGYAN.

Does MoneyGYAN offer investment advice?+

No. MoneyGYAN operates as an AMFI-registered Mutual Fund Distributor, not a SEBI-registered Investment Adviser. Guidance provided to partners and their clients is incidental to distribution, in line with AMFI's Code of Conduct, and should not be read as personalised investment advice.
